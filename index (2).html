For Sapna

There’s something I want to tell you.
Tap the lantern when you’re ready.

Dear Sapna,

I’ll say this plainly, because you deserve honesty more than pretty words.

I took the best things for granted: your patience, your laugh, the way you made ordinary days feel lighter.

I know I haven’t always made you feel like a priority. That was my failure, not yours, and I’m sorry.

I’m not asking you to forget what happened. I’m asking you to see who I’m working to become.

When I picture my future, the home, the family, the quiet evenings, it’s you beside me. Not as a wish, but as a promise I want to earn.

If you give me another chance, you will come first in my words and in my actions: in how I listen, how I show up, and how I speak to you and about you.

I want to do this the right way, with respect, with patience, and with our families when you feel ready.

Sapna, will you let me try again?
